# Privacy Policy for BillsHQ

**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Developer:** wjr (Independent Developer)

## 1. Overview
BillsHQ is a personal finance app designed for tracking recurring income, expenses, subscriptions, budgeting, and maintaining a checkbook. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the app.

BillsHQ does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or financial data with the developer or any third parties.

## 2. Information Stored by the App
BillsHQ stores only the data you explicitly enter so the app can function properly. This may include:

- Recurring Bills & Income: Name, notes, category, frequency, amount, due date, payment method (e.g., cash, bank account, credit card).
- Categories: Custom category names created by the user.
- Checkbook & Ledger: Transaction date, amount, check number, payee/payer details, memo notes, and transaction type (income, expense, or transfer).

All budgeting views, subscription metrics, and historical logs are derived strictly from this locally stored data.

All information is stored locally on your device and, if you choose, in your private iCloud account associated with your Apple ID.

## 3. iCloud Sync & Data Control
BillsHQ uses Apple’s secure iCloud service to sync your financial records across your personal iOS devices.

- Private Storage: Data is stored within your personal, encrypted iCloud account managed directly by Apple.
- Developer Access: The developer has zero access to or visibility into your iCloud storage, device data, or financial logs.
- Deleting Data: Removing the app deletes all local data from your device. You can manage or clear your synced app data at any time through your device’s Apple ID iCloud storage settings.

## 4. No External Data Collection or Analytics
BillsHQ does not:

- Collect personal identification or financial details
- Transmit any data to external servers or third-party databases
- Use third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising tools
- Request access to your device’s contacts, photos, or location

Your data stays entirely under your control.

## 5. Security
BillsHQ relies on Apple’s secure iOS environment. All data is stored using system-level protections provided by iOS and iCloud. No external servers or third-party services are involved.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
BillsHQ does not collect or transmit personal data from any user, including children under 13, in compliance with standard privacy regulations such as COPPA. The app is not directed toward children.
